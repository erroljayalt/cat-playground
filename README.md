# Cat Playground

A 3D animation built with [three.js](https://threejs.org/): two tabby cats, orange **Marmalade** and gray-and-black **Smokey**, playing together.

Open `index.html` in a browser to watch. It loads three.js from a CDN, so it needs an internet connection.

## Places

Use the **Place** row in the toolbar to switch between them:

- **Bedroom**: bed, wardrobe, desk, bookshelf, rug, cat bed and scratching post
- **Yard**: fenced garden with trees, flowers and a bench
- **Beach**: waves, palm trees, a beach umbrella, a towel and a sandcastle
- **Snowy garden**: falling snow, pine trees, a snowman and a lamp post

## What the cats do

On **Autoplay** they choose actions by themselves:

- Chase, bat and pounce on a ball of yarn
- Roll over and kick their paws in the air
- Get the zoomies and run around
- Chase their own tails
- Stretch
- Play tag with each other
- Nap in their favorite spot

You can also choose a cat (Marmalade, Smokey or Both) and press any action button. Drag to rotate the view, scroll to zoom, and click the ground to drop the yarn there.

## Files

- `index.html` is the whole project in one file: the scene, both cats and all of their behaviours.
